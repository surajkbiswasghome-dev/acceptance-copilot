# Manthan Acceptance Copilot: offline demo

Round 1 working prototype for the NPCI Lead Product Management (Acceptance / RuPay) role. All data is synthetic.

## Run it (no server, no password, no installation)
1. Click **index.html** above, then **Download raw file** (the download icon), or use **Code > Download ZIP**.
2. Double-click the downloaded `index.html`. It opens in Chrome or Edge.
3. "Demo admin" is pre-selected. Press **Continue to workspace**.

## Notes
- Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Email, voice, Telegram and AI-written explanations are switched off in this copy.
